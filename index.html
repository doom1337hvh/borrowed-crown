    Borrowed Crown

    One town, four hundred men, three rival houses. Take the realm. Hold it while you can. Nobody keeps it.

    • The landTurn it, zoom in, and watch your soldiers march, camp and fight.
    • OrdersTap one of your towns, then tap where its men should march.
    • MenLevied from your own people and fed on your own grain. Cut off, an army starves.
    • WallsThree times the garrison opens the gates. Otherwise starve it out, or storm it.
    • CrownRule the most people and it is yours. Then everyone comes for you.

    There is no victory screen.

    The crown passes on

    Your orders

    • SelectTap one of your towns, camps or columns. Double tap takes everything you have.
    • MarchThen tap the town to march on. Or drag from your men to the target and let go.
    • GatherPress and hold any town, then let go, to send the selection there. Your own towns too.
    • SeveralPress Several, tap each town or army you want, then tap the target.
    • LookOne finger drags the land. Two fingers pinch to zoom, twist to turn, drag to move.
    • SelectClick one of your towns, camps or columns. Shift-click adds one, a drag across open ground boxes several, A takes everything.
    • MarchThen click the town to march on, or drag to it. Right-click sends the selection to any town, your own included.
    • LookThe wheel zooms. Right-drag turns the land, middle-drag or the arrow keys move it. Q and E turn, H shows the whole realm, V the flat map.
    • Keys1, 2 and 3 set how many march. S storms or sallies. Space pauses, M mutes, Esc lets go.

    The bar below. March is the share of a garrison that goes. Levy is how hard you conscript: more men, fewer farmers, angrier towns. A red button appears when a camp can storm the walls or a besieged town can sally out.
